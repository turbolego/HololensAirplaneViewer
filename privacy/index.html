Privacy Policy for HololensAirplaneViewer

Last updated: August 5, 2026

Data Collection

HololensAirplaneViewer does not access, collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

  • The location capability is used exclusively to compute aircraft positions relative to the device — coordinates never leave the device.
  • The webcam capability is used exclusively for HoloLens spatial mapping and head tracking — no camera images are captured, recorded, or transmitted.
  • The microphone capability is used exclusively for HoloLens voice commands and spatial audio — no audio is recorded or transmitted.
  • The internetClient capability is used only to fetch public aircraft position data (ADS-B state vectors) from the OpenSky Network API.

Data Sharing

No data of any kind is shared with third parties. Aircraft positions displayed are public data provided by the OpenSky Network.
